Terry Pickett Associates

Terry Pickett & Associates is an organizational effectiveness consulting firm. It specializes in communication-based organizational development on the individual, team and systems levels.

It has provided professional consultation to international, national and regional business, professional and industrial organizations for over thirty-five years. The firm’s clients range from industrial manufacturing businesses to state and local government, including Fortune 100 firms, small municipalities, and personal services organizations.

The firm’s approach tailors programs and processes to fit a client’s specific needs. Extensive collaboration yields a customized, effective development process. The firm is proud of its record of achievement as measured in customer satisfaction and continuing relationships.
