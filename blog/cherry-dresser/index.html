Cherry Dresser

This is a house-warming gift for Kate and Ryan.  Mostly their design, but, as usual, many small changes happened along the way.  Mostly what they ordered, though.

It has self-closing drawer slides as a feature.  Looks delightful in their bedroom.  44x42x22 — solid cherry, veneered cherry 1/4:” ply for sides and drawer center inserts.  Finished with transparent shellac, then poly; paste wax for additional protection on top.
