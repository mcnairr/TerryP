Entertainment Center

Fresh from my dining room table top, I launched into a custom design entertainment center for our new home in Pagosa Springs, Colorado.  Using the same quarter-sawn white oak and stain to match the dining room table and other assessories, I built this unit to house CDs and stereo equipment, including a mini-computer.
I loved this project, although I built it in the basement and hadn’t quite measured the winding steps in our 100 year old house.  Thanks to Ryan (Kate’s Ryan) we got it into the livingroom in time to ship to Colorado,
